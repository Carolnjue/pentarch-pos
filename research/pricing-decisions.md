# Pentarch POS — pricing & packages (decided 9 Nov 2026)

Source: research/hardware-market.md (prices checked 9 Nov 2026, Kenyan sellers). Benchmark: Techlion KES 1,200/month or 12,000/year.

## Free trial
30 days, full system, on the client's own computer (no Pentarch devices). Started by the client online, or by a sales agent.

## Software subscription (after trial)
- KES 1,200 per month, or KES 12,000 per year (2 months free).
- Includes: unlimited products, users and devices in one branch, cloud backup & sync, updates, WhatsApp support (Mon–Sat 8am–6pm).
- Each extra branch: + KES 1,000 per month (KES 10,000/year).
- Payment: M-Pesa Paybill (number set in cloud settings), Account = business code PPxxxxx.

## Agent commission
- 20% of software payments for the client's first 12 months (e.g. KES 2,400 on a yearly plan).
- KES 3,000 per Complete Till package sold; KES 1,500 per Software + Setup package; KES 1,000 per Counter Kit.
- Paid by M-Pesa monthly once the client's payment has cleared.

## Positioning vs competitors
- Price matches Techlion (1,200/12,000), but includes: 30-day free trial on the client's own computer, live online demo, Kiswahili, every business type (shop, hardware, agrovet, chemist, boutique, butchery, restaurant/bar, salon, wholesale…), works fully offline, multi-branch, credit book, label printing, stock-take, anti-theft exceptions report, audit log, and Pentarch local support.
- eTIMS: receipts already carry KRA PIN and tax codes A–E; full eTIMS (OSCU through Pentarch cloud) is on the roadmap pending KRA integrator certification — do not promise "eTIMS certified" until done.
