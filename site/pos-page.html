Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. Selling, receipts, stock and reports all work offline because the system runs on your computer. Sales are kept safely on the till and upload to your cloud backup as soon as the connection returns.

How much internet does it use?

Very little. Syncing sends small text records, not pictures or video, so an ordinary data bundle or home Wi-Fi is enough. Internet is only needed for backup, sync between devices and branches, and updates.

Is my data safe, and who owns it?

Your data belongs to you. It is stored on your till and backed up to the Pentarch cloud, separated from every other business. You can download a full backup or export everything to CSV at any time.

If your subscription lapses, you can still open the system, view reports and export your records. We never hold your data back.

Is it KRA eTIMS compliant?

Receipts already carry your KRA PIN and the KRA tax code for every item (A to E), and the VAT report groups sales by tax code. eTIMS integration is on the way. We will tell every client when it is available and switch it on for you.

How does M-Pesa work with it?

The cashier records the M-Pesa transaction code with the sale. The system checks the code format and warns if the same code has been used before. Your till or paybill number prints on every receipt, and M-Pesa totals appear in the shift and sales reports. A payment request sent to the customer's phone can be set up on request.

What happens when the 30-day trial ends?

Choose a plan and pay by M-Pesa Paybill: KES 1,200 per month or KES 12,000 per year. Nothing is charged automatically. Everything you entered during the trial stays. If you do not continue, selling stops after a short grace period, but you can still view and export your records.

Can I run more than one branch?

Yes. Each extra branch is KES 1,000 per month (KES 10,000 per year). You get branch comparison, stock transfers between branches and per-branch prices, all under one account.

What support do I get?

WhatsApp and phone support from our Nairobi team, Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6pm, on +254 729 455 912. Updates are included in the subscription.

Will my staff be trained?

Yes. Both packages include training for up to 3 staff. The system also has a short guided tour for new cashiers, and every screen is available in English and Kiswahili.

Can I use my own computer?

Yes. You need a Windows PC or laptop with a Core i3 processor or better, 4 GB RAM and Google Chrome. USB barcode scanners and 58 mm or 80 mm thermal receipt printers work with it. Add our Counter Kit if you need the scanner, printer and cash drawer.
